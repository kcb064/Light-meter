Hierarchy: the solved variable (shutter in A-priority, aperture in S-priority) is a 48sp light display; the full exposure triangle sits beneath it in dim 14sp joined by middle dots. Held state turns the EV amber — the one place color signals state. Status microcopy is 12sp label under the value: HELD, settling…, unsteady light — reading may be off. Reciprocity corrections are amber (they matter — the film needs more time). Range clamps are error coral. Empty state is an em dash: EV —, waiting for a reading.
