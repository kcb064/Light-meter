The live preview is the only bright region on screen — everything around it stays near-black so the scene reads like ground glass. Spot mode: tapping drops an amber crosshair reticle (circle + ticks) where the meter samples; the reticle is the same amber as every other "active" signal. Average mode hides the reticle and meters the full frame. The viewfinder flexes vertically; readouts and dials below keep fixed heights so the whole meter fits without scrolling.
