Third Stop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Third Stop does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no network access at all — it does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of sending anything anywhere.

Camera

Third Stop requests the CAMERA permission for one purpose: to read the exposure level of the scene you point the phone at. Camera frames are analysed on your device, in real time, to determine the auto-exposure result. No photograph or video is ever recorded, saved, or transmitted. Nothing from the camera leaves your device or is written to storage.

Ambient light sensor

When you use incident metering, the app reads your device's ambient light sensor. These readings are used only to compute the exposure shown on screen.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, on your device only:

  • Readings you explicitly choose to save to the log.
  • Your settings — ISO, aperture, film stock, and calibration offsets.

This data is stored in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded. You can delete individual log entries in the app, and uninstalling Third Stop removes all of it.

If you have Android's system backup enabled, this local data may be included in your device backup to your own Google account, under Google's privacy policy. That backup is between you and Google — the developer of Light Meter has no access to it. You can turn this off in your device's backup settings.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or registration.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No location access.
  • No sharing or selling of data — there is no data to share.

Children

Third Stop is a photographic tool suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it collects nothing from children either.
